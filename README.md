  <h1>Privacy Policy for MidSpot</h1>

  <p>Last updated: April 29, 2026</p>

  <p>
    MidSpot is a location-based app that helps users find fair and convenient meeting places.
    This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information related to location, search,
    and third-party services.
  </p>

  <h2>Information We Collect</h2>
  <p>
    MidSpot may request access to your device location so the app can calculate a meeting point
    and show nearby places. You may also manually enter locations for yourself or others.
  </p>

  <h2>How Location Information Is Used</h2>
  <p>
    Location information is used to calculate meeting points, estimate travel distance or time,
    and search for nearby venues such as restaurants, bars, parks, or other places.
  </p>

  <h2>Data Storage</h2>
  <p>
    MidSpot does not require account registration. MidSpot does not sell your personal information.
    Location information is used to provide app functionality and is not sold.
  </p>

  <h2>Third-Party Services</h2>
  <p>
    MidSpot may use third-party services such as Apple Maps, Yelp, OpenTable, or Resy to provide
    maps, search results, venue information, reviews, availability, or reservation-related features.
    These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.
  </p>

  <h2>User Control</h2>
  <p>
    You can control location permissions through your device settings. You may disable location
    access at any time, though some app features may not work without location access.
  </p>

  <h2>Contact</h2>
  <p>
    If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: your-email@example.com
  </p>
